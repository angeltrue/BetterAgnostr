# Better Agnostr 1.1.11 — Brighter rainbow and scattered shooting stars

**This hotfix brightens `&g` rainbow text and makes shooting stars appear independently across visible rows.**

## Rainbow

- Added a screen-blended brightness lift to rainbow fills.
- Replaced the heavy dark shadow with a softer white glow and lighter edge shadow.
- Rainbow text is brighter without changing emoji colours.

## Stars flair

- Shooting stars now appear across 1–3 different visible flaired rows per trigger.
- Each star has a random delay, start position, angle, length, duration, and path.
- Stars can appear at different heights and horizontal positions across the screen.
- Targets are limited to rows visible in the viewport.
